APMS Help

APMS manages graduation projects from registration through supervision, evaluation, and final archiving.

  1. Activate your university account and sign in.
  2. Students register their project and team information.
  3. Supervisors review progress, feedback, and submissions.
  4. Coordinators organize sections and evaluation committees.
  5. The Head of Department reviews final results and archived projects.

Project files are submitted from the student dashboard after signing in.

  1. Open your project page and confirm the project and team details.
  2. Choose the required submission stage or milestone.
  3. Upload the requested report or supporting files.
  4. Review the file name and details, then submit before the deadline.
  5. Check notifications for supervisor feedback or required changes.
Student: registers the project, follows milestones, uploads files, and receives feedback.
Supervisor: supervises teams, reviews progress, provides feedback, and submits evaluations.
Coordinator: manages sections, registrations, schedules, committees, and course workflows.
Head of Department: assigns supervisors, reviews grades, manages users, and accesses the archive.
