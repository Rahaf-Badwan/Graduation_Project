ℹ Each professor's base teaching load is 12 credit hours/semester. Maximum allowed is 18 credit hours. Professors who take on Project Introduction sections or Graduation Project supervision receive an additional load, calculated as:

Extra Load = (# of Intro-Project sections) + (# of Graduation Projects supervised) + 1

Example: 2 intro sections + 3 graduation projects = 5 → +1 = 6 extra credit hours. At end of semester, Ruba applies this +1 addition to each professor's total.
